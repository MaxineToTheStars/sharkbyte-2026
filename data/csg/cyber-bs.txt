Foreign Language Competence *
* Foreign language is a graduation requirement for the baccalaureate met through 8 credit hours at the elementary 2 level in one foreign language or equivalent. Certain foreign language courses count towards program electives. Students may satisfy equivalence through standardized examinations or successful completion of two credits (two years) in one foreign language at the secondary (high school) level. For additional information, including exemptions for students whose native language is not English, see the Testing and Assessment Department.
